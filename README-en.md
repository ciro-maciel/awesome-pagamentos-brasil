## Standards

What the Banco Central and Febraban define.

- [Arranjos de pagamento](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-arranjos-de-pagamento) - Banco Central rules for cards, wallets and other payment arrangements. `official` `free`
- [Pix](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-pix) - The official Pix page at the Banco Central. `official` `free`
- [Pix Automático](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-pix-automatico) - The Pix mode for recurring charges, with prior authorization from the payer. `official` `free`
- [Nova Plataforma de Boletos](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-nova-plataforma-de-boletos) - Febraban's central registry of boletos (bank slips). `official` `free`

## Gateways and subacquirers

Card, boleto, Pix and subscriptions in one API. In alphabetical order.

- [Adyen](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-adyen) - Global payments platform, with card, Pix and boleto in Brazil. `API` `paid`
- [Asaas](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-asaas) - Charges by Pix, boleto and card, with subscriptions and split. `API` `paid`
- [dLocal](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-dlocal) - Payments in emerging markets, including Brazil. `API` `paid`
- [EBANX](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-ebanx) - Local payments in Latin America, for companies inside and outside Brazil. `API` `paid`
- [Efí](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-efi) - Former Gerencianet: Pix, boleto, card and carnê via API. `API` `paid`
- [Iugu](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-iugu) - Charges, subscriptions and split with Pix, boleto and card. `API` `paid`
- [Mercado Pago](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-mercado-pago) - Checkout, Pix, card, boleto and subscriptions from Mercado Pago. `API` `paid`
- [Pagar.me](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-pagar-me) - Payments by Pix, card and boleto, with recurring billing and split. `API` `paid`
- [PagBank](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-pagbank) - Orders and charges by Pix, card and boleto. `API` `paid`
- [Stripe](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-stripe) - Boleto, Pix and card at Stripe, with recurring billing via Stripe Billing. `API` `paid`
- [Vindi](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-vindi) - Recurring billing and subscriptions API. `API` `paid`
- [Zoop](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-zoop) - Payment infrastructure for marketplaces and platforms, with split. `API` `paid`

## Acquirers

Whoever processes the card. In alphabetical order.

- [Cielo](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-cielo) - Cielo's developer docs: e-commerce, payment link and card machine. `API` `paid`
- [Getnet](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-getnet) - Getnet's developer portal. `API` `paid`
- [Rede](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-rede) - Rede's developer portal, from Itaú. `API` `paid`
- [Stone](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-stone) - Stone's docs for payments and accounts. `API` `paid`

## Anti-fraud

To block fraudulent orders before shipping.

- [ClearSale](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-clearsale) - Fraud analysis for e-commerce orders. `paid`

## Boleto and CNAB libraries

To generate boletos and remittance files without depending on a gateway. Open source.

- [boleto.js](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-boleto-js) - Renders a boleto barcode as SVG, in JavaScript. `open source` `free`
- [Boleto.Net](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-boleto-net) - Boletos for Brazilian banks in .NET. `open source` `free`
- [BoletoNetCore](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-boletonetcore) - The Boleto.Net version for .NET Core. `open source` `free`
- [BoletoPHP](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-boletophp) - Boleto management system in PHP, open source. `open source` `free`
- [brcobranca](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-brcobranca) - Boletos and remittance and return files in Ruby. `open source` `free`
- [laravel-boleto](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-laravel-boleto) - Boletos, remittances and returns in PHP, for several banks. `open source` `free`
- [OpenBoleto](https://go.mymarketing.click/pagbr-openboleto) - Generation of bank slips (boletos) in PHP. `open source` `free`

## Tags

Each item says what it is. The tags next to it help you choose: official when it comes from whoever sets the standard, open source when the code is published, free or paid based on the usage model, API and SDK based on how you integrate. Items tagged "by the author" are made by Ciro Cesar Maciel and follow the same rules as all the others.
